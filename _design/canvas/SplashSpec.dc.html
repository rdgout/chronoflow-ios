Splash screen

Night turns to dawn while the app loads: the sun comes up and a flight path crosses the sky, then the splash lifts away into a loaded app.

No snap: the sky is shared, so only the moving parts change. The moon is the one element that carries over (1600–2300 ms, ease-in-out) and lands exactly on the Welcome wordmark. Welcome content enters 2300–3200 ms, 14 pt rise and fade, staggered about 100 ms. Signed-in users get the lift-away into Today instead.

Timing

0–300 msStatic first frame, matches the system launch screen
300–1100Dawn gradient rises; the moon lifts and settles smaller
300–1200Stars fade out
400–1300Sun rises over the horizon
500–1400Flight path draws left to right, a light travels along it
1000–1400"ChronoFlow" fades in
1500Animation done. Open as soon as the app is ready
+400Hand-off: splash fades and scales up 8% to reveal the app

Easing: ease-out for the sun and icon, linear for the path. Total before hand-off: 1.5 s.

Loading while it plays

Start loading at launch. The app reads the stored plan and starts a background sync the moment it launches, while the animation plays.
Never wait for the network. The app is offline first: as soon as the stored plan is read (usually well under 1.5 s), the splash hands off when the animation ends. Sync carries on in the background; Settings shows "Syncing…" quietly.
Never cut the animation short. Even when data is ready early, the splash finishes its 1.5 s and then hands off, so it never flashes.
Slow first load. Only when nothing is stored yet (first open after sign-in) can it take longer. At 2 s "Getting your plan ready" fades in under the wordmark.
Hard limit. At 8 s the app opens anyway, with skeletons where data is missing (the existing first-load boards).
Lands on. Today, or the screen a notification or link opened. Sign-in, terms update and walkthrough come after the splash when needed.

Platforms and settings

iOSThe launch screen (storyboard) can't animate: it is the night-sky colour with the amber moon centred, exactly the 0 ms frame. The app's first screen redraws that frame and plays the animation from there, so the switch is invisible.
Android 12+The system splash shows the moon (as the splash icon) on the night-sky colour. Keep it on screen until the first frame is drawn, then play the same animation in the app.
Reduce MotionNo sun, path or movement: the launch frame cross-fades into the app in 300 ms. The status line still appears on a slow first load.
Dark modeSame animation on the dark sky colours.
