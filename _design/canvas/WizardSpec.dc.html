New trip wizard

One wizard for guests and members: one question per screen, native controls. Updated 4 Nov: no drafts, quiet recovery, fixed From on the flight home, trip-wide date message.

Paths

  • Returning member, simple trip: Where (route) → Times → Coming back? (One way) → Checking → Fine-tune → Ready → Calculating.
  • With a return: … → Coming back? (Add my return flight) → Your flight home (route) → Its times → Checking → …
  • Longest path (first-time guest, 3 blocking questions): Where (route) → Times → Your sleep → Checking → Age → Shift work → Caffeine → Checking again → Fine-tune → Ready → sign-up sheet. Guests plan one-way trips only, so they never see Coming back? or Your flight home.
  • Your sleep shows only until a member has answered it once. "Not sure" counts as an answer. Blocking questions only appear when the server asks for them.
  • Fine-tune is skipped when the server suggests no extras. Ready's Extras row then reads "3 days prep" and opens Fine-tune with only preparation days and the switch.
  • Duplicate opens on Outbound: airports filled in, dates cleared, titled "New trip", no last-trip hint, sub line "Copied from Amsterdam → Tokyo. Choose the new dates." Coming back? is preselected when the original had a return.

No drafts, quiet recovery

  • There are no drafts. Closing discards the trip. × asks "Discard this trip?" only when something was entered; otherwise it closes at once. There is no "Keep draft", no draft to resume and no draft card anywhere.
  • Guests: the same alert, with "You go back to the welcome screen." × and the exits of the failure and No plan screens go to Welcome.
  • Edits: "Discard your changes?" The trip stays as it was. There is no "Save changes" link.
  • Quiet recovery: when the app is reopened within 30 minutes (for example after buying credits in Safari), the wizard opens on the same step with the same values. It is never shown or listed as a draft. It is deleted after 30 minutes, on × or after a save.
  • If the app was killed during the save, it reopens on Calculating and retries, with no extra message.

Header, progress and back

  • Full-screen modal over the tabs. Close is always the × on the right. From step 2 a back chevron sits on the left, plus swipe-back.
  • Progress is a bar that fills. When Checking adds questions, the bar keeps its position and the rest fills in smaller steps, so it never jumps back.
  • Back on Checking (chevron is hidden; swipe and Android back still work): cancels the check and returns to the last input step.
  • Back on the first question: the last input step, skipping Checking.
  • During the save (Calculating): × and back are hidden; swipe and Android back do nothing.
  • The main button sits above the home indicator and rises with the keyboard. Single-choice screens have no button: tapping an answer moves on after 250 ms, so the selection is seen. Taps are ignored until the next screen shows (double-tap guard), and a button disables on its first tap until the response.

Checking

  • One request. The screen stays up at least 1.2 s. Line 1 ticks at 0.4 s, line 2 at 0.8 s, line 3 when the response arrives (never before 1.2 s). After the last tick, a 0.3 s pause, then the next screen.
  • Reduce Motion: the dot on the arc stays put, ticks appear without scaling, the spinner becomes a static dot. Same timing.
  • Screen reader: the title is read on arrival. Each line is announced when it ticks: "Flights and time zones, done" / "Vluchten en tijdzones, klaar". The open line reads "What we can tailor, in progress" / "Wat we kunnen afstemmen, bezig".
  • Slow: after about 10 s: "This is taking longer than usual. You can keep this screen open." / "Dit duurt langer dan normaal. Je kunt dit scherm open laten." After 30 s it counts as a server error.
  • Re-checks: after the last blocking answer, "Checking your answers" with the progress bar held. After a change in Fine-tune, Continue shows a spinner ("Checking…") and opens Ready. An unchanged Fine-tune goes straight to Ready.
  • Trip-wide messages arrive after Checking, in the banner slot under the title of the last flight screen: Outbound on a one-way trip, Your flight home on a trip with a return. The slot takes the past-arrival message and any trip-wide server line (a planner rejection, "Try saving again shortly.", "This setting cannot be changed here.", too many attempts). Edits never get the past-arrival message. "Check the departure and arrival times." stays at the field.
  • Too many attempts: when the server gives a wait time, "Too many attempts. Try again in {count} seconds." and the button counts down ("Try again in 42 s"), enabled at 0. Without one, "Too many attempts. Please try again later." and Try again stays enabled.
  • Practice mode blocks Checking and saving like offline, with its own copy and "Turn off practice mode".

Back to Ready

  • From Ready (new trip, guest or edit), each row opens its step. Done or Continue on an unchanged step goes straight back to Ready.
  • A changed step goes through Checking, then back to Ready, skipping later steps unless one is now needed (a new blocking question shows first, then Ready).
  • A checked trip expires after 30 minutes. Ready checks again after buying credits, after quiet recovery, when it was left open past 30 minutes, or when an edited trip changed on another phone. The button shows "Checking…" and Ready stays. When the price or summary changed, a banner says what changed ("On another device: …" for changes made elsewhere) and the new values are shown.
  • A no-credits Ready checks silently when the app comes back to the front, and switches to the normal Ready once there are enough credits.

Haptics

  • Selection: a choice card, chronotype, answer, picker value, each stepper step, the switch.
  • Light impact: swap, add a stop, remove a stop.
  • Success: Checking finishes and the next screen appears; the plan is saved.
  • Warning: an inline error after Continue; a trip-wide message; No plan available.
  • Error: Checking or saving failed (offline, server, practice mode, too many attempts).
  • Medium impact: "Make my plan" and "Update my plan".

Decisions

  • Questions use the server's wording. Titles over 80 characters drop to a smaller size. One yes/no question per screen.
  • Editing opens on Ready ("Edit your trip"). "Update my plan" is disabled until something changes. Editing is free, except adding a return to a one-way trip: 1 credit. Removing the return gives no credit back; Ready shows "Free".
  • Route first, then times (Rick, 9 Nov). Where sets the whole route. Once From and To are picked it asks "Do you change planes on the way?" (No, direct / Yes). Yes opens the stop search; the stop appears as a row between From and To, with × to remove it. "Add another stop" adds the next one before the destination; the search names the two airports it splits. Choosing "No, direct" removes all stops. The swap button only shows on a direct route (swapping a route with stops is unclear). At 16 flights "Add another stop" is replaced by "That's the most we can plan: 16 flights."
  • Times: one card per flight, labelled with its route, with the connection or overnight line between cards (information only: stops are changed on Where). New flights start with empty pills. With three or more flights the times screen scrolls; Continue stays fixed, and an error after Continue scrolls to the first one. Field errors sit under the card they belong to.
  • Your flight home gets the same two steps: its route (From fixed to where the outbound landed, lock icon; the same stop question; To follows the outbound's origin until changed; no stops copied), then its times ("When do you fly home?", dates empty). There is no flying home from another airport; if the server rejects it anyway, its line "Your flight home must leave from the airport you landed at." shows in the banner slot.
  • Web trips that end after nights or on a check-out date: Coming back? shows that end as a third, selected choice ("Ends after 9 nights", set on the website). The Return row reads "9 nights · until Wed 21 Oct" or "Check-out · Wed 21 Oct". One way or Add my return flight replaces it; the app can't create one.
  • Reminders: after the first saved trip, a one-time sheet over the trip page, once Calculating is done. Only when notification permission was never asked and reminders are on. "Not now" means never again.
  • Errors at the field: a red outline on the pill or row and one line under it.
