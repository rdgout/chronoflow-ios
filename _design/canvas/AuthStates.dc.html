Sign-in error banners

One banner style for every sign-in screen, for errors that are not about a single field.

Placement

That email and password don't match.Check both and try again, or reset your password.
Form fields

The banner sits at the top of the white card, above the fields, and is announced to screen readers. Field problems (a short password, a wrong code) stay under the field, as on the existing boards. The banner clears when the user edits a field.
