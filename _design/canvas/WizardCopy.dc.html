Focus goes to the title on each new step. When a trip-wide message arrives, focus goes to it first. After Continue with errors, focus goes to the first error.

Android

  • × on the right, as on iOS: Rick's rule is one place for Close.
  • Date and time pills have no compact form: a tap opens the Material date or time dialog.
  • Preparation days: drawn − and + buttons (48 dp), no native stepper.
  • Progress: the native determinate linear progress indicator, styled like the 4 pt bar.
  • Pickers follow the app's language and the phone's 12/24-hour setting.

Layout

  • The step content scrolls; header, progress bar and main button stay fixed. On 390×844 nothing scrolls at the default text size; questions fit up to the largest standard size.
  • On 375×667: Fine-tune and Ready scroll; other steps fit.
  • Column at most 520 pt wide, centred (669 pt wide and up). Android tablets stay portrait for now.
  • Date and time pills: inline up to xxxLarge; from AX1 they wrap under the label, side by side, full width.
  • Ready label column: 92 pt up to xLarge; above that the label sits above the value.
  • Sleep's 2×2 grid becomes one column at AX1 and up, and under 360 pt wide.

Dutch terms

  • luchthaven (not vliegveld)
  • lichtbox (not lichtbak)
  • credit / credits
  • Tijd in {city} (not "Amsterdamse tijd"); Lokale tijd when the city is unknown
  • Where a board differs from the app's existing Dutch, the app wins.
