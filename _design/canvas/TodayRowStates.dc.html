Done and skipped steps stay where they are, so the day keeps its order and the user can see what they did. They leave Today when their time is over, like any past step. Progress counts done steps only.

Done: check in a filled dawn circle, dimmed swatch, muted title, "Done" first in the second line. Skipped: outlined skip icon and "Skipped". No strike-through, so titles stay readable; the icon and the word carry the state, not colour alone. Screen readers hear "Avoid bright light, done".

Changing your mind

Tapping a done or skipped row opens the step sheet with Undo, as on the Item detail board. There is no check control on the row itself: rows stay one large tap target, and checking off happens in the sheet where the user sees what the step was.

The web's check controls on the row are a mouse pattern; on the phone a small checkbox next to a row-wide tap target causes mis-taps.
